# Thesis Chapter Review

Review adversarially but constructively. Do not rewrite unless asked; point to location, problem, fix.

## Passes (in order)
1. **Reverse outline**: one-line summary per paragraph; flag paragraphs with no clear point, duplicates, jumps.
2. **Claim-evidence map**: main claims and the evidence/citation backing each; flag unsupported or overstated claims.
3. **Argument fit**: does the chapter serve its purpose statement and the RQs? Intro promises vs delivery? Terms and numbers consistent with other chapters and with the code/docs in this repo?
4. **Critique pass**: skeptical examiner. 5-10 hardest questions/objections, severity (major/minor), response strategy (fix, clarify, defend, add evidence).
5. **Citation check**: every in-text citation is in the reference list and vice versa; style matches (APA 7 default); never confirm a reference exists unless the source is available. Ask the user to confirm each source supports its sentence.
6. **Language**: clarity, concision, passive overuse, hedging, tense consistency. Audit for generic AI-sounding patterns (filler transitions, inflated vocabulary, uniform rhythm, vague abstractions); suggest plainer, specific wording in the author's voice.
7. **Format**: heading hierarchy, figure/table numbering and captions, campus template compliance.

## Output
Prioritized issue list (major first): location, problem, concrete fix. End with a 3-line readiness verdict and top 3 actions.

## Rules
- Author's argument and voice stay theirs. No invented sources or data. Remind about AI-disclosure rules.
